# Brown & Brown Customer Context

Load this skill when doing any Brown & Brown work: data modeling, project management, writing vibes for the modeling agent, or preparing deliverables.

## Company Profile

Brown & Brown (NYSE: BRO) is one of the largest insurance brokerages in the world.

- **Founded:** 1939 in Daytona Beach, FL (originally "Brown & Owen")
- **Headquarters:** Daytona Beach, Florida
- **Revenue:** $5.9 billion (2025)
- **Employees:** ~23,000 ("teammates")
- **Offices:** 700+ across 19 countries
- **S&P 500** member since 2021; S&P 500 Dividend Aristocrat since 2022
- **CEO lineage:** Adrian Brown (founder) -> Hyatt Brown -> J. Powell Brown (current)
- **Growth model:** Aggressive M&A -- hundreds of acquisitions since going public in 1993

## Business Segments

### 1. Property & Casualty (P&C)
The core brokerage business. Lines include:
- **Property:** catastrophe modeling, valuations, builder's risk, inland/ocean marine, political violence/terrorism
- **Casualty:** workers' comp, general liability, product recall, excess liability, auto liability
- **Cyber insurance**
- **Environmental liability:** pollution legal liability, remediation costs
- **Executive liability:** industry-specific (entertainment, financial institutions, healthcare, real estate)
- **Multinational liability**
- **Surety bonds**
- **Trade credit risk**
- **Capabilities:** casualty analytics, risk analytics and insights, loss control and safety, claims advocacy

### 2. Employee Benefits
- Benefits design and delivery (medical, dental, vision, life, disability)
- Financial strategy and underwriting analytics (utilization reviews, benchmarking)
- Population health and wellness programs
- Benefits administration technology (platform selection, integration, support)
- Pharmacy benefits management
- Voluntary and lifestyle benefits
- Medicare solutions
- Employer stop-loss
- Compliance and regulatory guidance
- Employee communications programs

### 3. Personal Insurance
- Homeowners, auto, umbrella
- Specialty: wildfire, flood, boats/yachts, private collections
- Family office and private client solutions
- High-net-worth individuals

### 4. Consulting
- **Actuarial services:** captive/self-funded programs, employee benefits, P&C, reinsurance, stop-loss
- **Healthcare claims audit:** claim adjudication, cost management, stop-loss reviews, plan document analysis
- **Health and welfare consulting:** benefit strategy
- **M&A consulting**
- **Pharmacy consulting**
- **Retirement benefits consulting**
- Team includes consultants, actuaries, data scientists, pharmacists, accountants
- All work is in-house (not outsourced); work product is client-owned

## Key Characteristics for Data Modeling
- **Decentralized operations:** 700+ offices means diverse local practices unified under corporate segments
- **M&A-heavy:** data integration from acquired companies is a persistent challenge
- **Multi-line:** a single client relationship may span P&C, benefits, personal, and consulting
- **Regulated:** insurance is heavily regulated at the state level (US) and by country internationally
- **Geographic scope:** US primary, plus Canada, Ireland, UK, expanding into Asia and Europe
- **Terminology:** employees are "teammates," offices are part of "team of companies"

## When Writing Vibes for the Modeling Agent
- Use insurance brokerage terminology, not carrier terminology (B&B places insurance, they don't underwrite it)
- The business is a broker/intermediary -- they connect clients to carriers and manage the relationship
- Revenue comes from commissions and fees, not premiums
- Key entities likely include: clients/accounts, policies, carriers/markets, producers/brokers, claims, commissions, submissions/quotes
- Consider the multi-segment nature: a "client" in P&C may also be a "client" in employee benefits
- M&A means expect to model acquisition tracking and entity consolidation
