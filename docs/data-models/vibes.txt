PHARMACY CLAIMS ARE THE CORE. Central fact: pharmacy claim transaction at fill/reversal/adjustment grain. Each claim ties to a member, drug (NDC), dispensing pharmacy (NCPDP ID), prescribing provider (NPI), and PBM. Financials: ingredient cost, dispensing fee, plan paid, member copay, coinsurance, deductible applied/remaining, OOP applied/remaining, other payer, total cost.

MULTI-TENANT HIERARCHY: Company > Division > Client. Every claim, member, and benefit group is scoped here. Members scoped to Company/Client, not Division.

MEMBERS are real people (patients). Same member may appear across PBMs with different IDs. Member 360 (unified cross-PBM view) is a primary objective. Demographics: name, gender, DOB, address, phone, relationship to subscriber, Medicare MBI.

SUBSCRIBERS are distinct from members -- primary policyholder; members are dependents. Often sparse data.

DRUGS keyed by 11-digit NDC. Medi-Span/First DataBank reference: AWP/WAC pricing, therapeutic class, generic indicator. Type-2 SCD.

PHARMACIES keyed by NCPDP ID. Type, chain/independent, specialty. Type-2 SCD.

PROVIDERS keyed by NPI. Prescribing provider is primary role. NUCC taxonomy.

PBMs (CVS, Optum, ESI, Navitus, EmpiRx, BMICH, Rightway, Prime, ServeYou, Carelon) each have different formats. Model must normalize across all into one canonical structure.

BENEFIT GROUPS: benefits configuration within a client. Type-2 SCD history. Carries line of business. Client fiscal calendar drives fiscal month/quarter/year.

COPAY ASSISTANCE (CAP): manufacturer copay cards, coupons, 340B. Drug type classification, program tracking, inclusion/exclusion indicators.

CLAIM STATUS: paid(+1), reversed(-1), adjusted/rejected(0). Track complete/orphaned reversals. Adjusted view nets adjustments into payments.

ADDITIONAL DOMAINS: prior authorization, manufacturer rebates, medical claims for total cost of care.

Role-playing date dims: fill, submit, load date. Full source lineage: PBM, file, format, durable business keys.
